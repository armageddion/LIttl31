Home Assistant made self-hosting mainstream. Nabu Casa made it convenient. ALFR3D is built the same way — a real platform running on hardware you own, with a hosted layer you can opt into, never the other way around.

A containerized microservices backend, a situational-awareness engine that actually knows what day it is and what's playing, and Nexus Launcher — an Android home screen built to talk to it directly.

/ NEXUS LAUNCHER

Nexus Launcher

A free-form spatial launcher for Android — no permanent dock, apps and ALFR3D surfaces open as free-floating windows on an infinite canvas. Double-tap search, a built-in terminal, live situational-awareness cards.

Free core, forever. A one-time unlock removes the ceiling — no subscription, because a launcher sitting on your own phone doesn't cost us a server bill to run.

One-time purchase through Google Play Billing — not a subscription, no tiers. Unlocks extra themes and icon packs, advanced window layouts (snap presets, multi-window save/restore), and layout backup & restore.
/ ALFR3D CORE

ALFR3D Core

The backend. Kafka messaging, containerized services, a dashboard that actually looks like something you'd want on a wall panel — not a spreadsheet.

Stays free forever, stays self-hosted, stays local-first. This is the platform, not the product we're selling — think of it the way Home Assistant thinks of Home Assistant Core.

ALFR3D Cloud

Coming — Phase 3

Benchmarked against Nabu Casa — a fair price for infrastructure we run, on top of a platform that costs nothing to run yourself.
